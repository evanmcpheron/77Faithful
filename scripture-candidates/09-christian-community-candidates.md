Week 9 — Christian Community

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                         | Purpose                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Formed into God’s People      | Establish that Christian community begins with God bringing people to Himself in Christ and giving them a shared identity as His household and people. |
| 2   | One Body, Many Members        | Explore belonging, diversity, mutual dependence, and grace-given roles within one body.                                                                |
| 3   | Devoted to Life Together      | Practice shared teaching, fellowship, prayer, worship, meals, and tangible care.                                                                       |
| 4   | Unity, Humility, and Peace    | Learn the character and practices required for reconciliation, truth, peace, and faithful handling of conflict.                                        |
| 5   | Carry One Another             | Bear burdens through encouragement, prayer, comfort, restoration, accountability, and presence in suffering.                                           |
| 6   | Build One Another Up          | Use gifts, teaching, hospitality, mentoring, leadership, and shared responsibility to strengthen others.                                               |
| 7   | A Community That Displays God | See holy, loving, persevering, diverse community as a visible witness to God and the gospel.                                                           |
