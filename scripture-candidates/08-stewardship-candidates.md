Week 8 — Stewardship

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day   | Focus                     | Purpose                                                                                                          |
| ----- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | God Owns and Gives        | Establish that every resource is God’s gift and that stewardship begins with gratitude, dependence, and worship. |
| Day 2 | Faithful Management       | Learn to manage entrusted resources attentively and faithfully under God’s authority.                            |
| Day 3 | Treasure and Contentment  | Examine money’s claim on the heart, reject covetousness, and learn contentment in God.                           |
| Day 4 | Work and Provision        | Connect stewardship to diligent work, wise planning, household provision, and responsible care.                  |
| Day 5 | Generosity and Open Hands | Practice willing, grace-shaped generosity toward needs, ministry, and the vulnerable.                            |
| Day 6 | Integrity and Justice     | Bring earning, spending, debts, obligations, and economic relationships under God’s righteousness.               |
| Day 7 | Kingdom Accountability    | View temporary resources through eternity, discipleship, and the certainty of giving account to God.             |
