Week 5 — Identity in Christ

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day   | Focus                  | Purpose                                                                                                                                     |
| ----- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | Belonging to Christ    | Establish that Christian identity is received through God’s call, new birth, and belonging to Jesus rather than status or achievement.      |
| Day 2 | Children and Heirs     | Explore adoption, intimacy with the Father, family identity, and inheritance through Christ and the Spirit.                                 |
| Day 3 | Redeemed and Made Holy | Ground identity in the cross: forgiveness, justification, reconciliation, sanctification, and grace rather than self-improvement.           |
| Day 4 | Alive in the Spirit    | Show that belonging to Christ includes the Spirit’s indwelling, sealing, life, assurance, and transforming presence.                        |
| Day 5 | God’s Own People       | Develop the corporate dimensions of identity: household, temple, priesthood, holy people, and Christ-centered family.                       |
| Day 6 | Walk as Who You Are    | Connect received identity to faithful conduct, holiness, public witness, and ordinary obedience without making behavior the basis of grace. |
| Day 7 | Hope and Inheritance   | End with security, resurrection, heavenly citizenship, inheritance, future likeness to Christ, and eternal communion with God.              |
