# 02 · Principles and Boundaries

[Documentation guide](README.md)

**Authority:** This document owns the permanent product principles. They apply to V1 and every later version.

## 1. Jesus is the focus; Scripture is central

77Faithful is explicitly Christian and biblically based. It is not a generic wellness program with religious language added afterward. Its practices, devotional content, and invitations to action should support following Jesus.

The product's grounding includes abiding in Christ (John 15:1–11), salvation by grace rather than earned works (Ephesians 2:8–10), prayer (Matthew 6:5–13), engaging and responding to Scripture (2 Timothy 3:14–17; James 1:22–25), and love of God and neighbor (Matthew 22:34–40). These references state the intended biblical basis; the app's particular schedule and checklist are human-created supports, not biblical commands.

Scripture must be visibly distinguished from 77Faithful's own commentary. A devotional is an aid to understanding and response, not an additional source of divine authority. Human-written prompts must never be presented as a new message spoken by God to that particular participant.

## 2. Broadly Christian, without obscuring the Christian message

The product welcomes Christians from different traditions and people exploring faith. It remains centered on Jesus, Scripture, grace, repentance, prayer, love, service, and perseverance.

It must not casually present disputed denominational practices as obligations for every Christian. Content concerning baptism, communion, church governance, spiritual gifts, end-times interpretations, or other disputed matters requires particular care. V1's curriculum does not need to resolve those disputes to fulfill its purpose.

Broadly Christian does not mean the app avoids Jesus, treats all religious beliefs as interchangeable, or replaces biblical teaching with motivational language. It means the product does not require a particular denominational identity to participate.

## 3. Faithfulness rather than perfection

Missing a practice, missing an entire day, or returning after a long absence never resets a journey to Day 1.

The app encourages honest participation. It must not tell someone to falsify their record, dismiss the value of effort, or suggest that failure to check a box means God has rejected them.

The product may communicate a real commitment and a clear expectation. Encouragement does not require pretending that an incomplete day was complete. The correct response is accurate information and an invitation to continue.

No screen may imply that app participation earns salvation, blessing, answered prayer, financial prosperity, or greater standing before God.

## 4. Five to seven daily practices

The daily commitment remains five to seven practices: three foundational practices and two to four selected additional practices.

A devotional, intention, reminder, weekly introduction, optional written entry, or completion summary must not become a sixth required practice. The product must not quietly add extra requirements through progress calculations or a completion button.

## 5. People declare their own participation

There are no spiritual completion timers, proof submissions, required photos, word-count tests, or examinations. The app does not judge the sincerity or quality of a prayer or journal entry.

The participant may mark and unmark an eligible day's practices. A reading does not need to be opened in the app to count. Writing can support reflection, but writing is not proof that reflection occurred.

The app should distinguish **not recorded as complete** from **known not to have happened**. It cannot observe a person's entire life.

## 6. Permanently free, without advertising

No participant-facing function may require payment, now or later. This includes Scripture access offered by the app, devotional content, reflection, reminders, history, community participation, community creation, group leadership, church use, and accessibility features.

There are no subscriptions, trials that expire into payment, premium translations, paid spiritual content, feature tiers, group-seat charges, advertisements, sponsored placements, or affiliate-driven recommendations.

Financial circumstances must never determine a person's access or standing in the product. Operational costs are not a reason to introduce paid access without changing a permanent owner commitment; under this baseline, that change is not permitted.

A voluntary support button may be considered in the future. It may not unlock anything, remove anything unpleasant, grant status, solicit payment during spiritual vulnerability, or imply spiritual obligation. See [Future vision](14-communities-and-future-vision.md).

## 7. Privacy is a product promise

Personal motivations, intentions, and reflections are private. In V1, there is no in-app sharing of those writings or of detailed journey records.

Future community membership must never automatically expose private content. Group organizers, family members, pastors, and donors have no special entitlement to read someone's personal journal.

A person must control any future sharing deliberately, understand the audience, and be able to withdraw the shared item from the app. The app cannot promise to recover information another person has already copied; that limitation must be explained honestly.

## 8. Accountability without competition

No points, levels, badges, achievement awards, leaderboards, spiritual rankings, follower counts, popularity scores, or comparative faithfulness scores are part of the product.

A small personal complete-day streak is permitted as a factual account of the person's own recorded days. It is subordinate to the journey and must never become the main reason to participate. No streak freezes, purchases, punitive alerts, or rewards are allowed.

Future group progress must support encouragement, not expose who is least faithful or pressure members to disclose more.

## 9. Accessibility and ordinary life matter

People should be able to use the app with larger text, assistive reading, limited dexterity, different physical capacities, and unfamiliarity with mobile products.

Physical movement is not measured against a universal exercise target. Generosity does not require money. Fasting is never compulsory, and selecting a discipline practice does not require abstaining from food. Family-oriented practice must not imply that unmarried people or people without children have a lesser form of faith.

No practice may require a person to ignore their health, personal safety, responsibilities, or another person's boundaries.

## 10. The app supports, but does not replace, Christian life

77Faithful is not a replacement for Scripture itself, participation in a local church, pastoral care, Christian community, or thoughtful personal discernment. It is also not professional counseling, medical treatment, or an emergency response service.

The app must not encourage someone to remain in danger, stop appropriate care, disclose trauma to strangers, reconcile in unsafe circumstances, or treat an app prompt as authoritative direction for a major life decision.

## 11. Honest limits and truthful presentation

Do not call content available before it can actually be read. Do not call writing saved when it remains at risk. Do not claim a named translation is included before the appropriate permissions and reading experience are established.

Do not describe an early-ended journey as a completed 77-day journey. Do not suggest that a calendar-based journey represents 77 perfect days. Do not tell participants that their private journal is being monitored for pastoral or emergency support.

## What 77Faithful is not

77Faithful is not a spiritual competition, a holiness score, a prosperity promise, a public social network, a replacement pastor, an unrestricted Bible-study library, a medical fasting program, a paid devotional service, or a system for proving one's faith to a group leader.

It is a practical structure for engaging Scripture, praying, reflecting, acting intentionally, loving others, and continuing in faith.
