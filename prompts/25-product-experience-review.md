## Evaluate the experience

- Check Scripture's prominence/readability and the fallback when text is unavailable.
- Walk progress, completion, reminders, and return after missed/partial days for consistency with faithfulness over perfection. For the required V1 complete-day streak, evaluate subdued Journey-only placement and absence of spiritual-worth or pressure framing; distinguish missing implementation from scope decisions.
- Check agency: reversible self-reported practices, optional journaling, and the contract's explicit reflection-completion actions. Typing or draft autosave is not completion; `Save & mark reflection complete` and `I reflected without writing` are deliberate actions.
- Inspect privacy cues, previews, consent, and sharing defaults for reflections, journals, intentions, and prayer content. Community remains Future with no V1 entry points or placeholders; when explicitly in scope, check encouragement versus popularity/competition and item-specific sharing consent independent of membership.
- Compare screen responsibilities, next actions, missed/current/history/future-day behavior, and the Day 77 return experience with the contract. Do not redesign navigation for aesthetic preference; a warranted architectural recommendation identifies the necessary contract update and any intentional change to settled policy.
- Consider participants with different schedules, caregiving/work responsibilities, and physical abilities. Identify unnecessary pressure, time demands, physical requirements, or barriers to resuming participation.

## Verify and report

Walk supported states such as a missed day, return after absence, journey completion, and unavailable Scripture. Tie each issue to exact copy/interaction, a documented product principle, participant impact, and a bounded correction. Distinguish visible privacy cues from backend guarantees requiring separate verification. Stop at concrete product mismatches; do not turn this into theological adjudication or redesign for preference.
