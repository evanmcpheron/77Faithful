# 10 · Progress, History, and Reflections

[Documentation guide](README.md)

**Authority:** This document owns progress presentation, personal statistics, historical browsing, written records, and journey summaries. Document 04 owns day eligibility and calendar behavior.

## Progress should answer practical questions

The participant should be able to tell which day they are on, which practices they have marked complete, which days they can review, and what they previously wrote.

Progress does not answer how holy someone is, whether God is pleased with them, or whether they have prayed sufficiently. Those meanings must not be implied by a number, color, label, or chart.

## Information hierarchy

### Today

The most prominent information is **Day X of 77**, the current theme, the assigned reading, and **X of 5 practices complete**. The participant should not have to find a statistics page to understand what to do next.

Streaks are not the main Today headline. A participant returning after an absence should see the current reading and invitation to continue, not a dominant record of loss.

### Journey

Journey provides a 77-day overview and a readable chronological list alternative. The overview distinguishes completed, partially recorded, unrecorded, current, future, and not-reached days using words or accessible labels as well as visual treatment.

A participant can inspect their own progress without being compared with anyone else. Future days are present for orientation, not access to their detailed content.

### Supporting statistics

V1 may show the following defined statistics in the journey's secondary progress area. In this baseline, these modest statistics are included; they do not create a separate analytics experience.

| Statistic                   | Exact meaning                                                                                                              | Required qualification                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fully completed days        | Number of eligible days with all five practices currently marked complete.                                                 | Does not mean those were the only spiritually meaningful days.                             |
| Practices marked complete   | Total completion markers across eligible days in this journey.                                                             | Counts declarations, not observed or evaluated spiritual acts.                             |
| Current complete-day streak | Consecutive fully completed days at the current end of the record, using the rule below. Shown only for an active journey. | Based on recorded days, including later edits; not verified on-time completion.            |
| Longest complete-day streak | Longest consecutive run of fully completed days within this journey.                                                       | A personal historical fact, not an award or rank.                                          |
| Written reflections         | Number of days with a saved, nonempty reflection.                                                                          | Does not include an intention-only entry, a draft, or repeated edits; writing is optional. |

V1 does not show a spiritual score, a weighted practice score, a completion percentage as a measure of faith, or a comparison percentile. No cross-journey lifetime streak is created.

A complete 77-day journey has 385 possible practice markers because each of 77 days has five practices. During an active journey, simple completed counts avoid implying that future days are already overdue. At an early ending, summary denominators include only reached days; unused days are not portrayed as missed opportunities the person had already passed through.

## Exact complete-day streak behavior

If today's five practices are complete, the current streak counts consecutive complete days ending today.

If today is not yet complete, an existing streak through yesterday remains visible while today is still open. An unfinished morning does not immediately break a streak. If yesterday was not complete, there is no continuing current streak to preserve.

When a new day begins, an incomplete previous day interrupts the run. This does not interrupt the journey itself. Day 1 with no completed day has a current streak of zero.

Historical changes recalculate the streak from the current record. The app must therefore explain that streaks describe recorded completed days, including later updates. It must not label them as proven consecutive days of on-time behavior.

After a journey completes or ends early, its summary may show its longest run but does not continue a live streak beyond the journey's eligible dates.

### Examples

| Situation                                                                     | Result                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Days 1–4 are complete; Day 5 is still in progress.                            | The current streak remains 4 during Day 5.                                        |
| Day 5 becomes complete.                                                       | The current streak becomes 5.                                                     |
| Day 5 stays incomplete and Day 6 begins.                                      | The current streak becomes 0 until a new complete day begins a new run.           |
| Day 6 becomes complete while Day 5 remains incomplete.                        | The current streak is 1.                                                          |
| The participant later completes Day 5's record and Days 1–6 are all complete. | The recorded current streak becomes 6, with the historical-update qualification.  |
| A complete-day marker is accidentally cleared and then restored.              | Statistics reflect the current corrected record; no permanent penalty is imposed. |

No alert says that the participant will lose God's favor, lose their entire journey, or disappoint a group by interrupting a streak.

## Day states in the overview

| State                                     | Suitable label                     | Interaction                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Current, no practices marked              | Today · 0 of 5                     | Open today's experience.                                                       |
| Current or historical, partially complete | 3 of 5 practices complete          | Open and continue or update the day.                                           |
| Current or historical, fully complete     | All five practices complete        | Open and review; corrections remain possible.                                  |
| Historical, no practices marked           | No practices marked complete       | Open and review or update without a failure label.                             |
| Future within an active journey           | Available on [date]                | Explain the date; do not reveal detailed content.                              |
| Beyond an early ending                    | Not reached · journey ended early  | Explain that these days did not become part of the reached history.            |
| Temporarily unavailable on this phone     | Connect to make this day available | Explain availability without calling it an empty or missing historical record. |

Calendar color alone must never be the only way to understand these distinctions.

## Browsing previous journeys

Journey includes a clear choice of the active journey and previous journeys. Every journey has understandable dates and a status: active, completed, or ended early.

A historical journey should not be identified only as **Day 12**, because another journey may also have a Day 12. Screens and reflection entries include enough journey context to distinguish them, normally the start date and status.

Opening an old journey does not replace the active one or change Today. The participant has a clear return to the active journey.

Completed journeys expose all 77 days. Early-ended journeys expose reached days through the ending day and distinguish the rest as not reached. No historical record becomes a fresh active journey merely because it is edited.

## The Reflections destination

V1 has a dedicated **Reflections** destination for the participant's saved day-based writing. It includes days containing a saved intention, a saved reflection, or both. Each entry makes its type clear; an intention-only entry must not be called a written reflection in the statistics.

The default view contains all journeys in newest-assigned-date order. A simple journey filter lets someone focus on the active journey or one previous journey. Entries associated with the same calendar date retain clear journey labels; the newer journey can be listed first when a tie needs ordering.

The list shows the assigned date, journey day, theme or title, a short private text preview, and whether the entry was updated later. An edit to an old day does not silently move it to the top as though it were today's reflection; its original date remains primary.

Selecting an entry opens its day or reflection view in the correct journey. The participant can review the related Scripture and prompt, edit their words, or delete the relevant writing.

### Empty state

A participant can complete Reflect without writing. An empty collection therefore says **Your written intentions and reflections will appear here** and may explain that writing is optional. It must not state that the person has never reflected.

An intention can exist without any practice being marked complete. It is still legitimate saved writing.

### What is not included in V1

There is no full-text search, tag system, folders, mood chart, automatic interpretation, sentiment score, favorite collection, voice recording, photograph attachment, or separate prayer-request history.

The participant may manually use ordinary text-selection or copying capabilities where available. A dedicated export or printable journal feature is a possible later addition, not a V1 promise.

## Writing, saving, editing, and deletion

Writing is free-form text with no spiritual-quality assessment, word-count requirement, or mandatory structured answer. Reasonable length limits may protect usability, but they must be visible before text is rejected and must not be framed as a spiritual target.

A deliberate save action confirms that writing has been retained. The interface distinguishes unsaved draft text, text saved on the current phone, and text safely available to the account where that distinction matters.

When the participant tries to leave with unsaved text, offer **Save**, **Keep editing**, or **Discard changes**. Do not silently lose text. Restored drafts must remain recognizable as drafts until saved.

Deleting a saved reflection or intention requires confirmation that names the writing being removed. The participant may delete one without deleting the other. Removal does not automatically change practice completion or erase the day itself.

If all day-based writing is deleted, the day disappears from the Reflections list while remaining in Journey. The written-reflection count changes only when the saved reflection changes from nonempty to empty or vice versa, not on every edit.

### Starting motivation

The optional starting motivation belongs to the journey as a whole. It appears in journey details and the completion summary rather than being counted as a daily reflection. It can be reviewed, edited, or deleted separately, with later edits identified accurately.

## Completion summary

Once the 77-day period ends, the summary should begin with **Your 77-day journey has ended** or a similarly accurate statement. It may acknowledge the participant's engagement without assuming they completed every practice or experienced a particular spiritual transformation.

The summary includes the journey dates, the fact that the period completed, fully completed days out of 77, practices marked complete out of 385, the longest complete-day streak, and the number of written reflections. These are secondary to the invitation to look back and continue in faith.

The participant can revisit their starting motivation and Day 77 reflection, browse earlier entries, return to the full journey, and start another journey when ready. The summary may invite them to consider what practices and relationships to continue outside the app.

There is no required final survey, certificate, badge, public share card, ranking, payment, or donation prompt.

The summary updates when historical records change. It is a view of the current personal record, not an immutable certificate claiming observed behavior.

## Early-ended summary

An early-ended summary begins with **Journey ended early** and states the actual end date and reached day count. It summarizes only the reached portion. For example, a journey ended on Day 20 may show fully completed days out of 20 and practices marked complete out of 100.

It retains private writing and provides access to the reached days. It does not present the unvisited remainder as 57 failed days. It offers a separate new journey without demanding that the participant justify the earlier ending.

## Progress privacy

In V1, all progress is personal. None is visible to other participants, churches, groups, or contacts. The existence of a future community plan must not lead a designer to add a public comparison, share button, or leader view to these screens.
