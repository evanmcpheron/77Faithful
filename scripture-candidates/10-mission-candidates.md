Week 10 — Mission

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day   | Focus                       | Purpose                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | Sent by Christ              | Ground mission in Jesus’ authority, saving purpose, and call to participate in what he is doing.                                  |
| Day 2 | The Gospel We Carry         | Clarify the Christ-centered message entrusted to believers: cross, resurrection, repentance, forgiveness, and salvation by faith. |
| Day 3 | Empowered to Witness        | Depend on the Holy Spirit and prayer for direction, courage, clarity, and power rather than self-reliance.                        |
| Day 4 | God’s Heart for People      | Learn to see the lost, outsiders, cities, and the harvest with God’s compassion and saving concern.                               |
| Day 5 | Witness in Word and Life    | Join truthful proclamation with wisdom, humility, love, holiness, service, and credible everyday conduct.                         |
| Day 6 | Make and Multiply Disciples | Move from initial proclamation toward teaching, maturity, sending, partnership, and reproducible discipleship.                    |
| Day 7 | All Nations Before the Lamb | Place personal mission inside God’s biblical purpose to gather worshipers from every people through Christ.                       |
