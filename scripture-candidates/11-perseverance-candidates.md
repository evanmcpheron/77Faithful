Week 11 — Perseverance

Translation: World English Bible (WEB)  
Candidate Count: 70  
Final Passages Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                        | Purpose                                                                                                   |
| --: | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Fix Your Eyes on Christ      | Begin with Jesus as the source, pattern, and goal of lifelong perseverance.                               |
|   2 | Let Trials Mature Faith      | Learn to face testing without pretending suffering is good in itself, trusting God to form endurance.     |
|   3 | Stand Firm Under Pressure    | Hold to Christ and the gospel through temptation, opposition, uncertainty, and spiritual resistance.      |
|   4 | Depend on God’s Strength     | Replace self-reliance with grace, the Spirit’s power, God’s care, and confidence in his faithfulness.     |
|   5 | Keep Practicing Faithfulness | Continue prayer, obedience, doing good, patience, and disciplined response when results are slow.         |
|   6 | Help One Another Endure      | Receive and give encouragement, correction, prayer, comfort, and strengthening within the body of Christ. |
|   7 | Finish in Resurrection Hope  | Carry faithfulness beyond Day 77 toward Christ’s return, resurrection, and the new creation.              |
