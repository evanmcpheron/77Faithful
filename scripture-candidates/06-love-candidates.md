Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day   | Focus                   | Purpose                                                                                        |
| ----- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | Love Begins with God    | Ground the week in God’s character and initiating love, especially as revealed in Christ.      |
| Day 2 | Love God Wholly         | Show love as a grace-shaped response of trust, allegiance, worship, and obedience.             |
| Day 3 | Love as Jesus Loved     | Look to Jesus’ command, compassion, self-giving example, and cross as the pattern of love.     |
| Day 4 | Love Those Near Us      | Apply love to neighbor, family, friendship, marriage, and fellow believers.                    |
| Day 5 | Love in Concrete Action | Move from sentiment to mercy, forgiveness, humility, truth, and deliberate good.               |
| Day 6 | Love Beyond Comfort     | Extend love to enemies, outsiders, difficult people, and situations that require costly mercy. |
| Day 7 | Mature in Love          | Integrate love as an enduring, growing, governing virtue of faithful Christian life.           |
