Week 1 — Abiding in Christ

Translation: World English Bible (WEB)  
Candidate Count: 70  
Final Passages Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                         | Purpose                                                                                                                                    |
| --: | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|   1 | Christ the Source             | Begin with Jesus himself as the source of spiritual life and fruitfulness; abiding is dependence on Christ, not self-produced improvement. |
|   2 | Come and Know Christ          | Move from doctrine to relationship: come to Jesus, hear him, follow him, and value knowing him above every competing gain.                 |
|   3 | Receive His Life              | See that Christ gives what the soul cannot manufacture—eternal life, living water, light, fellowship, and the Spirit.                      |
|   4 | Depend on Grace               | Ground ongoing life with God in Christ’s finished work, indwelling presence, mercy, and sustaining grace rather than personal merit.       |
|   5 | Remain in Trust and Obedience | Show that abiding expresses itself through continuing faith, responsive obedience, love, repentance, and attentiveness to God.             |
|   6 | Bear Fruit from Communion     | Connect inward communion with outward transformation: fruit, holiness, love, service, and new life flow from union with Christ.            |
|   7 | Continue Rooted in Christ     | End by calling the believer to keep seeking, looking to, growing in, and remaining near Christ beyond the first week.                      |
