# 07 · Eleven-Week Formation Plan

[Documentation guide](README.md)

**Settled product structure:** Eleven themes in the order shown, with seven days per theme, for 77 calendar days.

**Editorial status:** The daily titles, Scripture references, and focus statements below are a complete proposed outline for one fixed V1 course. They are not a finished devotional manuscript or a claim of publication approval. The owner has adopted the theme order; the precise daily selections require the biblical and editorial review described in Document 19 before release.

This is not a menu of alternate readings. If approved, each row supplies the one corresponding numbered day's direction. Participants do not see future daily details merely because this planning document contains the full course.

The sequence moves from relationship with Christ toward Scripture, prayer, renewal, identity, relationships, service, stewardship, community, mission, and continuing faithfulness. It is an organizing progression, not a ranking of spiritual maturity. Earlier practices continue while a new theme receives emphasis.

## Weekly and daily outline

## Week 1 · Abiding in Christ · Days 1–7

**Purpose:** Begin with relationship and dependence rather than performance.

**Direction for the weekly introduction:** Introduce the journey as a response to Jesus, not a way to earn His attention. Help the participant make room for Scripture and prayer without suggesting that a flawless schedule is the source of spiritual life.

| Day | Working title | Proposed assigned Scripture | Formation focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Remain in Christ | John 15:1–11 | Depend on Jesus as the source of spiritual life rather than treating the checklist as the source. |
| 2 | Make room to listen | Luke 10:38–42 | Notice competing demands and consider how to give attentive presence to Jesus. |
| 3 | Receive His invitation | Matthew 11:25–30 | Consider trust, humility, and the rest Jesus offers without promising a trouble-free life. |
| 4 | Seek God intentionally | Psalm 63:1–8 | Bring longing and attention to God in the circumstances of an ordinary day. |
| 5 | Make space for prayer | Mark 1:35–39 | Consider Jesus' withdrawal to pray without turning one time of day into a universal rule. |
| 6 | Continue as you began | Colossians 2:6–7 | Connect daily practices with continuing in Christ, gratitude, and dependence. |
| 7 | Trust the Shepherd | Psalm 23 | Review the week through trust in God's presence, including difficult circumstances. |

**Direction for the seventh day's reflection:** Where did you make room to be with Jesus this week, and where do you want to return to dependence on Him?

The weekly review belongs within that day's normal Reflect practice. It is not an additional required task.

| 12 | Respond, not only listen | James 1:19–25 | Choose a practical response while avoiding the suggestion that works purchase God's favor. |
| 13 | Bring Scripture into daily life | Deuteronomy 6:4–9 | Consider ordinary opportunities to remember and discuss God's Word. |
| 14 | Receive guidance for the next step | Psalm 119:97–105 | Review how Scripture has shaped attention, understanding, and action during the week. |

**Direction for the seventh day's reflection:** What did you learn from Scripture this week, and what response is worth continuing?

The weekly review belongs within that day's normal Reflect practice. It is not an additional required task.

| 20 | Continue praying | Luke 18:1–8 | Explore perseverance without treating repeated prayer as a way to control God. |

## Editorial review of the complete sequence

Review the course as a whole for repeated ideas, excessive reading length, missing context, denominational assumptions, insensitive applications, and an imbalance between personal formation and love of others. Repeated biblical themes can reinforce learning, but deliberate repetition should not become filler.

Review each passage against the intended devotional instead of forcing the passage to support an unrelated theme. A problematic reference should be corrected before publication rather than defended solely because it appears in this draft.

The fixed eleven-week order remains the product structure unless the owner explicitly changes it. Editorial refinement of a day's title or passage is not permission to add competing tracks, new daily obligations, or future content-selection features to V1.

## What participants can preview

Before and during the journey, participants may view the eleven theme names, day ranges, and brief theme descriptions. Full weekly introductions become available when their week begins. Detailed future daily readings, devotionals, and prompts remain unavailable until their assigned days.

Completed journeys retain access to the entire course they reached. Early-ended journeys retain only the reached portion; the remaining day rows stay unavailable in that historical journey.
