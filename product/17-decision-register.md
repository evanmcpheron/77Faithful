# 17 · Decision Register

[Documentation guide](README.md)

This register makes the origin of product decisions visible. It separates the owner's explicit commitments from the working defaults chosen under the owner's instruction to use recommendations for remaining product questions.

**Adopted defaults are requirements in this baseline.** They are not a list of unresolved questions. The owner may deliberately revise them, but revisions must be reflected in their owning documents and affected workflows.

The register is an index of decisions, not a second source of conflicting rules. Detailed subject documents control the exact behavior.

| D02 | Fix the journey to the phone's time zone at its confirmed start. | Keeps dates and history stable when the participant travels. | [04](04-journey-and-calendar.md) |

## External release conditions, not invented facts

| Condition | What must be established | What this baseline does not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scripture permissions and final catalog | Approved use for each offered text and the promised reading experience; explicit owner decision on any reduced catalog. | That every named translation is already cleared or available. |
| Finished formation course | Approved manuscripts and passage text for all 77 days and eleven introductions. | That the editorial outline or sample is a publication-ready course. |
| Account eligibility and safeguarding | A clearly adopted age/region policy and any necessary parental requirements. | That every age can legally hold an independent account everywhere. |
| Privacy and retention | Accurate disclosures, deletion process, justified exceptions, and actual retention periods. | That a complete legal policy has been drafted or that every copy disappears instantly. |
| Support and publishing identity | Real contact information, responsible content review, and truthful About information. | That a support team, live website, or response-time promise already exists. |

These conditions require real approval or confirmation. They do not reopen settled product choices or justify including unfinished promises in participant-facing screens.
