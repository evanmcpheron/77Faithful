Week 4 — Renewal

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                           | Purpose                                                                                          |
| --: | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|   1 | Return and Repent               | Recognize spiritual drift honestly and turn back to God because of his mercy and truth.          |
|   2 | Made New by Grace               | Establish renewal as God's saving work through Christ and the Holy Spirit, not self-improvement. |
|   3 | A New Heart and Spirit          | See God as the one who cleanses, revives, and changes the inner person.                          |
|   4 | Renewed Mind and Desires        | Let God's truth reorient thinking, desires, priorities, and perspective.                         |
|   5 | Put Off the Old, Put On the New | Translate inward renewal into concrete repentance, holiness, and changed patterns of life.       |
|   6 | Walk by the Spirit              | Depend on the Holy Spirit for ongoing sanctification, fruitfulness, and faithful growth.         |
|   7 | Daily Renewal and Restoration   | Continue forward in hope, trusting God to restore, strengthen, and finally make all things new.  |
