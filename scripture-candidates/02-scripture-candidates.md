## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day   | Focus                | Purpose                                                                                                                            |
| ----- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | God Has Spoken       | Establish Scripture as God-breathed, true, enduring, and situated within God’s revelation that reaches its climax in Jesus Christ. |
| Day 2 | Receive the Word     | Approach Scripture with humility, readiness, honesty, and a heart willing to respond to what God has spoken.                       |
| Day 3 | Understand the Word  | Read carefully, seek God-given understanding, respect context, and recognize how Scripture bears witness to Christ.                |
| Day 4 | Dwell in the Word    | Store, meditate on, remember, and weave God’s words into the ordinary rhythms of life.                                             |
| Day 5 | Do the Word          | Move from hearing and understanding to concrete obedience shaped by grace and love for God.                                        |
| Day 6 | Discern by the Word  | Test teaching, resist distortion, distinguish God’s word from human additions, and handle biblical truth accurately.               |
| Day 7 | Continue in the Word | Develop a lifelong pattern of reading, learning, teaching, proclaiming, and persevering in Scripture.                              |
