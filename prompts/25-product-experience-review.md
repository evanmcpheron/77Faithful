# Product experience review

## Role

Act as a product reviewer evaluating a completed 77Faithful feature against its spiritual-formation goals.

## Task

Feature or user flow: `<FEATURE>`

Intended participant outcome: `<REQUIREMENTS>`

## Context gathering

Read root and applicable directory `AGENTS.md` instructions, relevant product decisions in `docs/engineering/architecture-decisions.md`, and the design guide. Inspect implemented content, labels, metrics, interactions, data visibility, and relevant tests/runtime evidence within the flow.

## Success criteria

Identify concrete product/UX mismatches that affect participant understanding, agency, continued participation, or trust.

## Constraints

This is a product review, not theological adjudication. Do not edit source, content, tests, configuration, or documentation unless explicitly asked. Avoid abstract philosophical objections or preference-based redesign.

Use synthetic journals/reflections for observation. Evaluate selected product semantics rather than inventing a different journey model.

## Review expectations

- Check that Scripture remains central and usable without distracting ornament or engagement pressure.
- Review progress, missed/partial days, completion messages, and reminders for consistency without perfectionism, shame, spiritual-worth scoring, or competitive ranking.
- Check that streaks, if present, remain subordinate to continued participation and do not contradict the selected no-reset journey behavior.
- Evaluate user agency, reversible self-reported practices, optional journaling, and whether the interface implies that writing is required to complete reflection.
- Verify that private reflections appear private and are not exposed through previews, sharing defaults, or public metrics. Respect the selected first-release sharing scope.
- Look for manipulative engagement, unnecessary urgency, unclear consent, or penalizing users with different abilities or schedules.
- Rank concrete issues as Critical, High, Medium, or Low. Include the exact screen/state/copy, participant impact, proposed change, and implementation scope.

## Verification

Walk relevant states such as a partial day, missed day, return after absence, journey completion, or unavailable Scripture where supported. Distinguish visible UX evidence from backend privacy guarantees requiring separate verification. Recommend focused copy/interaction tests for actionable findings.

## Final response

Give prioritized product issues and practical corrections, or state that the reviewed experience aligns with the goals. Report changes/files changed (normally none), checks performed and outcomes, and remaining concerns.
