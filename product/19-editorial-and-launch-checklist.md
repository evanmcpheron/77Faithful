# 19 · Editorial and Launch Checklist

[Documentation guide](README.md)

This document identifies what must be true before 77Faithful is presented to participants as a complete V1 product. It is a non-technical publishing and product-readiness checklist, not an implementation plan or a claim that these conditions have been met.

## 1. Product definition is understood

The person reviewing release readiness should be able to explain the app without referring to an earlier project or private conversation. They should understand the distinction between completing the period, completing a day, and growing spiritually.

Confirm that the intended release matches Document 03 and the adopted defaults in Document 17. An excluded future feature should not be advertised as part of V1. A required feature should not be silently removed because it was described in a detailed chapter rather than the overview.

Any deliberate product change should be recorded in the owning document and reconciled with the relevant workflows, pages, and acceptance scenarios.

## 2. The full formation course is ready

| Material | Required quantity and quality |
| --- | --- |
| Course structure | Eleven themes in the adopted order, seven days each, totaling 77 days. |
| Assigned readings | A reviewed Scripture assignment for every day, with context and appropriate reading length. |
| Scripture text | Accurate full assigned passages in every translation actually offered, from clearly identified approved editions. |
| Devotionals | Seventy-seven original or appropriately authorized, reviewed daily devotionals. |
| Prayer prompts | Seventy-seven focused prompts aligned with their readings and themes. |
| Reflection questions | Seventy-seven primary questions, with each week's review integrated into its seventh day. |
| Intention invitations | Seventy-seven optional invitations to a grounded daily response. |
| Weekly introductions | Eleven reviewed introductions explaining the week's place in the course. |
| Final-day content | A Day 77 reading and reflection that support continuing beyond the journey without spiritual grading. |
| Help and practice descriptions | Accurate explanations of all ten optional practices, required practices, no-reset behavior, and privacy. |

Document 07 provides a 77-day editorial outline. Document 18 provides a sample. Neither fulfills the requirement for a complete approved course.

Before release, name who is responsible for biblical and editorial review. Review must include theological accuracy, context, plain language, permissions, and sensitivity around health, grief, family life, forgiveness, and spiritual authority. This document does not appoint an unnamed pastor or claim that an external reviewer has already approved the material.

## 3. Scripture availability is truthful

For each translation offered, establish the exact edition, the source of the approved text, the basis for permission to use it, the intended audience and regions, required acknowledgments, and whether the promised use without internet is permitted.

Record what has actually been approved. Do not write “Used by permission” as a claim of a permission that has not been established. Do not assume that a general quotation allowance automatically covers every part of the proposed app experience.

The desired V1 catalog contains NASB 2020, NIV, NLT, ESV, The Message, KJV, NKJV, CSB, and WEB. If some cannot be offered, the owner must explicitly approve and document the narrower release catalog before the app is described as ready. The app must never show unapproved translations as working choices or offer them behind payment.

At least one approved translation and all 77 days of assigned Scripture must be genuinely available for the promised reading experience before journeys are offered. A reference-only fallback during an exceptional availability problem does not replace this release requirement.

If a future donation arrangement, export feature, audio feature, expanded distribution, or other use changes the conditions of a permission, review that change before offering it. This is particularly important because publisher classifications of permitted use are not determined solely by whether participants pay.

## 4. Account eligibility, privacy, and support are settled

Publish a real privacy explanation consistent with the product's actual behavior. Establish account eligibility, intended release regions, any age-related or parental requirements, retention periods, deletion handling, justified exceptions, and a working support contact.

Do not invent a legal age threshold, claim universal worldwide eligibility, promise immediate erasure of every disconnected copy, or imply that private journals are monitored for emergencies. These matters require real release decisions and truthful disclosures.

Provide an appropriate route for reporting a product problem or a concern about formation content without requiring the participant to expose their personal journal. Do not promise live pastoral support or a response time without an actual service supporting that promise.

The associated domain supplied by the owner is not, by itself, evidence that a help center, website, organization, or staffed ministry service is already operating.

## 5. The participant experience is complete

Use [Product acceptance scenarios](16-product-acceptance-scenarios.md) to review the experience from a non-technical standpoint. In particular, verify first use, a normal day, reflection without writing, partial completion, missed days, historical changes, practice replacements, travel, early ending, Day 77, and another journey.

Confirm that every page has understandable behavior for empty information, an unfinished action, a failed action, unavailable internet, and a genuinely completed action. These must not all appear as the same blank or waiting screen.

Verify that the current-day number is not confused with the count of complete days. Verify that the Day 77 period ends without a compulsory final button and that Day 77 remains available afterward.

The app must not confuse saved text with a completed practice, a private reflection with a shared prayer request, or an early ending with deletion.

## 6. Reliability and privacy promises hold

Confirm that a prepared journey can be used without internet as described in Document 12. A person should not discover on Day 30 that only the first week's Scripture was actually ready.

Check that saved changes survive normal closing and reopening, that availability on another phone is represented truthfully, and that competing private writing is not silently discarded.

Confirm safe behavior for signing out with phone-only work, changing accounts, recovering access, deleting writing, and requesting account deletion. A different participant must not inherit private information or reminders.

The participant should not need to understand the app's internal operation to decide whether their work is safe.

## 7. Accessibility is part of release readiness

Review the main workflows with larger text, assistive reading, light and dark appearance, limited precision in touch, and little familiarity with mobile apps.

Check long Scripture passages, writing with the keyboard open, completion controls, historical day selection, save warnings, and account-deletion explanations. A readable day list must provide an alternative to the visual calendar.

Review practice examples for different abilities, financial situations, household arrangements, and schedules. No practice should become unsafe or inaccessible through an unnecessarily rigid amount or method.

## 8. Free access and scope are preserved

No advertisement, sponsorship, paid feature, trial, subscription, premium translation, payment requirement, or donation request is present in V1.

No unfinished community feature is advertised or inserted into navigation. A future-community description may appear in the product documentation without turning into a nonfunctional participant-facing promise.

No certificate, badge, ranking, spiritual score, conversion quota, public streak, or popularity feature has been introduced under the label of engagement or accountability.

## 9. Documentation status is honest

This directory establishes the intended product. It does not certify a finished app, signed Scripture agreements, legal review, published manuscripts, an active support service, or a released translation catalog.

An item belongs in a “ready” claim only after the corresponding evidence exists. Record incomplete conditions plainly rather than converting a plan into a statement of delivered behavior.

## Official reference notes for Scripture-use review

The following primary sources were consulted on **September 8, 2026**. They support the need to review actual permissions and acknowledgments. They do not prove that 77Faithful has obtained permission, and they do not select a way to provide Scripture inside the app.

### NASB and the Lockman Foundation

The Lockman Foundation's published guidance places conditions on NASB quotation and includes acknowledgment requirements. Review the actual intended use rather than treating the desired inclusion of NASB 2020 as an existing grant of permission.[^lockman]

### NIV and Biblica

Biblica distinguishes general quotation guidance from other licensing arrangements and addresses how use is classified, including fundraising-related uses. A permanently free participant experience must still be checked against the permission applicable to the actual product.[^biblica]

### NLT and Tyndale

Tyndale publishes NLT quotation guidance and a route for other permission requests. Its stated print and eBook allowance is not, by itself, evidence that this particular in-app reading experience has been approved.[^tyndale]

### ESV and Crossway

Crossway publishes conditions for ESV use and permission requests. Those conditions require review for the intended reading and distribution experience; naming the ESV in the product catalog does not establish that review has happened.[^crossway]

### WEB and eBible.org

The World English Bible publisher describes the text as public domain while identifying the World English Bible name as a trademark. The exact edition, text integrity, naming, and presentation should therefore still be checked rather than treating “WEB” as an unspecified interchangeable text.[^web]

These notes are deliberately limited. They do not provide a complete rights analysis for every intended translation, determine eligibility in every territory, or replace permission from the relevant rights holder where needed. The other requested translations need their own appropriate review before being offered.
