# Week 3 — Prayer

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) Candidate Count: 70 Final Passages
Needed: 7

## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                        | Purpose                                                                                                          |
| --: | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Access to God                | Establish prayer as gracious access to the Father through Christ, with the Spirit helping our weakness.          |
|   2 | Learning to Pray from Jesus  | Let Jesus shape prayer’s motives, priorities, petitions, forgiveness, and confidence in the Father.              |
|   3 | Honest Prayer and Repentance | Practice humility, confession, lament, and truthful pouring out of the heart before God.                         |
|   4 | Asking and Depending         | Bring real needs to God with persistence, trust, and dependence rather than self-sufficiency.                    |
|   5 | Surrender and Discernment    | Seek God’s will, guidance, and sustaining grace when desires, decisions, or suffering are difficult.             |
|   6 | Intercession and Mission     | Turn prayer outward toward other people, leaders, the church, enemies, and the spread of the gospel.             |
|   7 | A Life of Prayer             | Integrate prayer into a steady rhythm of watchfulness, gratitude, perseverance, worship, and daily faithfulness. |
