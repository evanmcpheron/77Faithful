# 12 · Reminders and Reliable Use

[Documentation guide](README.md)

**Authority:** This document owns reminder behavior and what participants can expect when connection, account access, or saving is interrupted. It deliberately describes outcomes rather than how they are achieved.

## Reminders serve the participant

V1 offers two optional reminder types: a morning invitation to begin with Scripture and prayer, and an evening invitation to reflect. Either, both, or neither may be enabled. The participant selects the times; there is no mandatory daily schedule.

The morning and evening names explain their purpose, not required hours of participation. Someone working nights can choose appropriate times. A chosen time does not become a deadline for spiritual completion.

Reminders are off until the person deliberately enables them. The phone's permission request follows that choice, not the first launch of the app. Permission denial does not block any journey practice.

### Scheduling and scope

Reminder times follow the local clock on the phone where they are enabled. Journey dates continue to follow the fixed journey time zone. Settings explains this distinction, especially for travel.

Reminder settings apply to that phone. Another phone does not begin notifying the participant merely because they sign in to the account there. They may enable reminders there explicitly. Signing out stops personal journey reminders on that phone.

Within a day, the app should not deliberately send duplicate copies of the same reminder. If the participant chooses the same time for both types, the interface should explain the overlap and offer a single combined reminder rather than two simultaneous alerts.

Reminders apply only to an active journey. They stop after an early ending or completion becomes known on that phone. Beginning another journey can reuse an existing enabled preference on the same phone, with the reminder state visible at review; previously disabled reminders are not silently reenabled.

### Completion-aware behavior

A morning reminder need not appear when the phone already knows that Scripture and prayer are both complete for the current journey day. An evening reflection reminder need not appear when Reflect is already complete. All reminders for the day can be suppressed when all five practices are complete.

A disconnected phone may not yet know about a change made elsewhere. For that reason, reminder wording stays neutral and must not claim that the participant definitely failed to pray, read, or reflect.

Missed notifications are not delivered later as a backlog. There are no hourly escalation messages, streak-loss warnings, guilt-based notifications, or pressure to catch up on every previous day.

### Suitable messages

**Morning:** “Your daily Scripture and prayer are ready.”

**Evening:** “Take a moment to reflect on your day.”

**Combined:** “Make time for today's reading, prayer, and reflection.”

Notifications do not contain private journal excerpts, intentions, names of people mentioned in writing, or detailed missed-practice counts. They do not request money.

Selecting a reminder opens the correct account's current Today experience after access is established. It does not permanently return the participant to the day on which a delayed reminder was originally created.

### Limits should be clear

The app can respect preferences and prepare reminders, but it must not guarantee that every phone will display every reminder at an exact second. Phone settings and interruption controls can affect what appears. The participant should have a simple way to check whether reminders are enabled without being told that a missed alert represents a spiritual failure.

## An established journey should work without internet

After an active journey is confirmed and its material is prepared on a phone, the participant should be able to continue the core personal experience without a connection.

That includes reading the assigned Scripture in the prepared selected translation, viewing the devotional and prompts, opening current and reached days in that journey, marking practices, saving private writing, and reviewing the saved current-journey record.

The phone must have the current journey's full 77-day formation material ready for this promise to be made. Future daily detail remains unavailable until its journey day arrives; preparing the course does not grant preview access.

The app clearly distinguishes **Journey started** from **Ready to use without internet**. Preparation should not be described as complete while some assigned passages or guidance remain unavailable. A newly signed-in phone must establish readiness separately.

### What requires a connection

Creating or confirming an account, signing in on a new phone, recovering account access, confirming a new journey, confirming an early ending, changing the optional-practice pair, making a new unprepared translation available, changing account contact information, and permanent account deletion require a connection in V1.

The app explains this before the participant believes an action succeeded. Existing daily participation is not blocked merely because one of these account-wide actions is temporarily unavailable.

### Historical availability

The prepared active journey's reached days remain usable without internet. Previous journeys and their writing are available without internet on a phone only after that history has been made available there.

A new phone with no previously available history must not portray temporarily unavailable records as nonexistent. It should explain that a connection is needed to obtain them.

A completed journey already available on a phone remains readable there; the passage of Day 77 must not suddenly remove its prepared reading material.

## Saving must be trustworthy

When a participant saves a practice change, intention, reflection, or motivation, the app must say accurately whether the change is retained on the current phone and whether it is safely available through the account on another phone.

Useful labels include **Saved on this phone**, **Waiting for a connection**, and **Saved to your account**. These are not interchangeable. The display should be calm and unobtrusive during ordinary use but clear when the participant is about to take an action that could lose work.

Work confirmed saved on the phone must survive normal closing and reopening of the app. Reading another day, changing appearance, or temporarily losing the connection must not remove it.

When the connection returns, saved changes should become available to the same account without requiring the person to retype them or repeatedly select a “send” command. Information must never migrate into a different person's account.

If the app cannot retain a change even on the current phone, it must not claim success. Preserve the visible text or choice when possible, explain the problem, and offer an understandable retry or a way to copy the person's own writing before leaving.

### Drafts and interrupted writing

Text being edited is a draft until saved. Ordinary navigation away from an unsaved draft offers Save, Keep editing, or Discard changes. A restored draft is labeled as such rather than presented as an already saved account entry.

An unexpected interruption may leave a recoverable draft. When available, it should be offered back in the correct journey and day. The app must not guarantee that unsaved text was retained when it was not.

A midnight change does not move the draft into the next day's entry. A translation change does not replace the participant's words.

## Multiple phones and competing changes

Signing in to the same account on another phone should show the same account's journey and protected records once they are available. It must not create a duplicate journey or present a new empty account as the existing record.

Independent changes should remain independent. Marking Movement complete must not replace the participant's reflection. Editing an old day must not overwrite Today.

When incompatible edits to the same writing exist on different phones, the app must preserve both versions and let the participant choose which to retain or compose a combined version. It must not silently delete one version of intimate writing or combine sentences in a way that changes their meaning.

A phone may also have older information about an optional-practice change confirmed elsewhere. When it learns the confirmed pair for an affected day, it must not silently transfer completion from a replaced practice to a different practice. Show the correct assignment, preserve the earlier recorded action for the participant to review, and let them correct the affected markers. Unrelated foundational practices and writing remain intact; the day still has exactly five assigned practices.

If a journey was ended early on another phone, a disconnected phone may not know immediately. Once it learns the ending, it must show the correct ended state rather than extend or reactivate the journey. Any writing already saved on dates beyond the reached portion must be offered privately for recovery or copying, not silently discarded or presented as valid newly reached journey days. Confirmed account deletion is different: recovery must not recreate information the participant has deliberately asked to remove.

For contradictory completion choices or settings, the app should reflect the latest clearly confirmed participant intention. If that intention cannot be established safely, it must make the disagreement visible and allow a correction rather than silently reviving an older state. This is a behavior requirement, not a prescription for how disagreement is detected.

## Signing out with work that exists only on the phone

The participant receives a clear warning before work saved only on that phone would be lost. They can remain signed in and reconnect so the work is protected for the account, or explicitly discard those unprotected changes and sign out.

Do not silently discard, attach the work to the next account, or say it is available on another phone when it is not. Once signed out, the former participant's private pages and reminder content are not visible to the next person.

A phone that loses account access for another reason should protect unshared work while explaining how the original participant can reestablish access. It must not expose that work to an unrelated account or treat access recovery as permission to erase it.

## Deletion and disconnected phones

Permanent account deletion requires a confirmed connected action. A disconnected phone may not learn immediately that the account was deleted elsewhere. The privacy explanation must not promise instantaneous removal from every disconnected phone or from copies someone already made outside the app.

Once the deletion is known on a phone, the app must stop access to the deleted account's private experience and prevent older saved changes from recreating it. Document 13 defines the deletion disclosures that must be finalized before release.

## Useful unavailable states

| Situation                                | What the person should understand                                    | What remains possible                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No connection during normal prepared use | The journey can continue; changes are currently saved on this phone. | Core daily reading, practice recording, and writing.                       |
| No connection on a newly signed-in phone | This phone does not yet have the necessary journey material.         | Clear explanation and retry when connected; no false empty history.        |
| Translation change cannot finish         | The replacement is not yet ready.                                    | Continue using the current available translation or a personal Bible.      |
| A save cannot be retained                | The action did not safely complete.                                  | Preserve the visible work, retry, or copy writing when possible.           |
| Start confirmation is uncertain          | The app is checking whether the journey was created.                 | Avoid duplicate starts; present the confirmed existing journey when known. |
| Work differs between phones              | Both versions exist and need an intentional choice.                  | Review and retain the appropriate writing without silent loss.             |

A participant should never need to understand the app's internal workings to decide what to do next.
