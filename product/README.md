# 77Faithful — Product Documentation

**Purpose:** Explain exactly what 77Faithful is, who it serves, and what a participant should experience.

**Edition:** Initial product baseline · September 8, 2026  
**Scope:** The complete personal V1 experience, permanent product principles, and clearly separated future community features.

77Faithful is a completely free, biblically based Christian spiritual formation app for anyone who wants to develop a stronger relationship with Jesus. Each participant follows a 77-calendar-day journey with five to seven daily practices: read Scripture, pray, reflect, and complete two to four additional practices chosen from a provided list. Eleven weekly themes guide the experience. Missing a practice or a day does not erase progress or restart the journey.

## How to read requirement language

**Permanent principle** means a boundary that applies to V1 and every future version. For example, no part of the app may require payment or contain advertising.

**V1 requirement** means part of the intended first participant release. A feature is not optional merely because it appears in a detailed document rather than the overview.

**Adopted default** means a product choice made where the owner explicitly delegated the recommendation. It is the working requirement for this baseline, not an unanswered question. These choices are identified in the decision register so the owner can review them easily.

**Future — committed direction** means the owner wants the capability, but it must not be treated as V1 work. Private communities fall into this category. No release date or numbered future release is promised.

**Future — possible** means an idea may be considered later. It is neither a V1 requirement nor a delivery commitment. Scheduling a future start and an optional donation button are examples.

**Editorial outline / illustrative example** means draft content showing the intended formation experience. It still requires the review described in Document 19 before publication. An example does not create a new feature or overrule a product rule.

**Launch condition** means an external fact, permission, policy, or editorial approval that must be established before the associated experience is offered. A desired Bible translation is not represented as already available merely because it appears in these documents.

## Product authority and responsibility

This directory is the primary authority for the product and its participant-facing behavior. Other product descriptions and designs must agree with it. Later decisions about how the app is built must support this experience rather than quietly changing it.

Document 02 owns permanent principles. Document 03 owns release scope. Each specialist document owns its subject; the table above identifies those subjects. Other documents summarize or illustrate those rules rather than creating competing definitions. Document 17 records the decisions and their origin; it does not silently override the specialist documents.

When a conflict is discovered, identify the exact statements, preserve the owner's explicit commitments, and correct the related documents together. Do not invent an exception in one screen description. Where two owner commitments genuinely conflict, obtain a product decision rather than treating an implementation limitation as permission to redesign the product.

## The product in one page

| Question                               | Answer                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is it?                            | A guided, personal, 77-day journey of Christian spiritual formation.                                                                                                 |
| Who is it for?                         | Anyone seeking a stronger relationship with Jesus, including people beginning, returning to, or growing in faith.                                                    |
| What does someone do every day?        | Read the assigned Scripture, pray, reflect, and practice two to four selected additional commitments.                                                                |
| What guides the content?               | Eleven biblical themes, each lasting seven journey days.                                                                                                             |
| How does it begin?                     | The participant chooses two to four practices, selects an available Bible translation, reviews the commitment, and selects **Start my journey**. That date is Day 1. |
| Must everything happen inside the app? | No. The app guides and records participation; Scripture may be read in a personal Bible and practices take place in everyday life.                                   |
| What happens after a missed day?       | The calendar continues. Previous progress remains. The participant may return to earlier eligible days and update their record.                                      |
| What does completion mean?             | The 77-day period has ended. This is distinct from completing all assigned practices on every day.                                                                   |
| Is the journal public?                 | No. Personal writing is private. There is no in-app sharing in V1.                                                                                                   |
| Is it free?                            | Yes, permanently. No advertisements, subscriptions, paid features, paid content, or paid community access.                                                           |
| Are groups included?                   | Private communities are a definite future direction, explicitly not V1.                                                                                              |
| What happens afterward?                | The participant retains their history, can review what they wrote, and may begin another separate journey.                                                           |

## A few important distinctions

A **complete day** means every practice assigned to that day has been marked complete. A **completed journey** means the full 77-day period has elapsed. Neither is a judgment of the person's spiritual maturity or God's favor.

The **Reflect practice is required**, but **writing in the app is optional**. The same distinction applies to daily intention: the app offers it, but it is not a sixth commitment.

The **eleven-week theme order is settled**. The detailed 77-day passage outline is an editorial draft, not a claim that all daily manuscripts and Scripture permissions are publication-ready.

The **desired translation catalog** and the **catalog actually approved for release** are not the same thing until permissions and the promised reading experience have been confirmed.

The product is designed for personal participation in V1. A person may practice alongside family or friends without the app containing group accounts, shared progress, or community features yet.
