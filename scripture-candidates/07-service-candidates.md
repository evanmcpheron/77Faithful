## Recommended Seven-Day Progression

| Day | Focus                 | Purpose                                                                                                 |
| --: | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Christ the Servant    | Begin with Jesus’ own example and self-giving as the pattern and source of Christian service.           |
|   2 | Serve the Lord        | Establish service as grateful allegiance, worship, and response to God rather than a way to earn favor. |
|   3 | Humble Readiness      | Confront status-seeking and cultivate willingness to take the low place and become useful to God.       |
|   4 | Gifts and Work        | Use God-given abilities, strength, and ordinary labor faithfully for the good of others.                |
|   5 | Practical Mercy       | Move from intention to concrete care, justice, hospitality, advocacy, and response to real needs.       |
|   6 | Strengthen Others     | Learn to support, equip, bear burdens, share responsibility, and build other people up.                 |
|   7 | Faithful Continuation | Keep doing good with perseverance, trusting God sees the work and that labor in the Lord is not wasted. |
