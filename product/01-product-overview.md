# 01 · Product Overview

[Documentation guide](README.md)

## What 77Faithful is

77Faithful is a free Christian spiritual formation app that helps people intentionally spend time with God and practice their faith through a structured 77-day journey. It is for anyone who wants to develop a stronger relationship with Jesus.

Each day combines Scripture, prayer, reflection, and practical action. The participant always follows three foundational practices—**Read Scripture, Pray, and Reflect**—and chooses two to four additional practices that fit their circumstances and areas of growth. This creates five to seven daily commitments without requiring everyone to live the same schedule or express their faith in identical ways.

The content follows eleven weekly themes. Each theme builds on the previous themes, beginning with abiding in Christ and continuing through Scripture, prayer, renewal, identity, love, service, stewardship, Christian community, mission, and perseverance.

The app provides guidance, reminders when requested, an honest record of participation, and a private place to reflect. It does not claim to produce spiritual maturity simply because someone follows a checklist.

The product name is **77Faithful**. The owner has identified **77faithful.com** as its owned associated domain. This product definition does not imply that a public website or support service is already operating.

## The purpose

The mission combines three closely related aims:

1. Help people develop consistent Christian spiritual practices.
2. Help people intentionally order more of their daily lives around Christ.
3. Provide a structured, accessible 77-day formation journey grounded in Scripture.

A participant should leave the experience better equipped to continue spending time with God and loving others beyond Day 77. Beginning another journey is available, but indefinite dependence on the app is not the objective.

## Who it serves

The intended audience includes people new to Christianity, long-standing Christians, people returning after a period of distance, and people exploring what following Jesus means. Participation does not require passing a theology test, belonging to a particular denomination, or disclosing personal spiritual struggles.

The tone and design should also work for people who are not confident using mobile apps. Someone should not need familiarity with Christian terminology to understand a practice description. Familiar terms such as Scripture, prayer, and reflection receive clear explanations where useful.

The product welcomes people with different physical abilities, family responsibilities, work schedules, financial circumstances, and amounts of available time. There is no implication that a parent with limited time, a disabled participant, or someone under significant stress is less faithful because they choose a different expression of a practice.

The spiritual invitation is broad. Independent account eligibility, particularly for younger participants, must still be described responsibly before release; see [Privacy, safety, and accessibility](13-privacy-safety-and-accessibility.md). Being unable to offer a particular account arrangement must never be presented as a statement about someone's welcome to Jesus.

## What a participant commits to

| Daily practice   | What it means in ordinary life                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read Scripture   | Engage with the day's assigned Bible passage, in the app or in a personal Bible. Accessible ways of receiving the passage count. |
| Pray             | Intentionally speak to God and make room for attentiveness to Him, with a focused prompt available for guidance.                 |
| Reflect          | Consider what the passage and the day's experiences mean for one's response to God. Writing is available but not compulsory.     |
| Chosen Practices | Carry out two to four distinct additional practices chosen from the catalog.                                                     |

There are no app-imposed minimum minutes, exercise distances, donation amounts, fasting durations, or word counts. Guidance should be concrete enough to help a person act without pretending the app can determine the quality of their spiritual engagement.

The participant marks completion honestly. Opening a reading, leaving a page on the screen, or typing a few words never automatically declares spiritual participation complete.

## A typical day

The participant opens **Today** and sees their current journey day, the week's theme, the day's reading, and the daily practices. They may set a short intention for how they hope to respond to what they read.

They read the Scripture inside the app or use the reference to read in their own Bible. A short devotional offers context and application without replacing the passage. The prayer prompt offers a direction for prayer. The participant marks practices complete as they engage in them.

The other practices happen in ordinary life: encouraging a friend, moving in a way that suits their body, memorizing Scripture, or another selected activity. Later, they return to reflect. They may write a sentence, several paragraphs, or nothing at all. Reflection can still be marked complete when done thoughtfully without writing.

The day shows honest progress, such as **3 of 5 practices complete**. There is no score for holiness, no comparison with other people, and no instruction to restart because the day was imperfect.

## How the journey progresses

The day the participant selects **Start my journey** is Day 1. The journey then follows 77 consecutive calendar dates in the journey's fixed time zone. It is not a count of 77 perfect days or 77 visits to the app.

When a day is missed, the following date still becomes the next numbered day. Previous days remain available to review and update. The participant can catch up voluntarily, but catching up is never a condition for accessing today's content.

At the end of the full 77-day period, the journey becomes completed regardless of the number of complete days. The summary distinguishes the completion of the period from the participant's recorded practices. It invites gratitude, honest reflection, and continuing growth rather than issuing a spiritual grade.

The participant may end a journey early. That history is retained and labeled accurately. It is not called a completed 77-day journey. They may start a new, separate journey when there is no active one.

## What makes this different from a generic habit tracker

Scripture is the center of the daily experience, not decoration around a checklist. The reading, devotional, prayer prompt, reflection question, and weekly theme are intentionally related.

The purpose of recording participation is accountability and recollection. A participant can look back and remember what they were learning and how they responded. The app does not attach spiritual value to accumulating points, protecting a streak, writing long entries, or spending more time on a screen.

Customization is deliberate but limited. The two to four additional practices allow the journey to fit real circumstances, while the shared foundation keeps it recognizably the same formation experience.

## Why 77 days

Seventy-seven days is the product's chosen duration. It provides eleven seven-day periods for a sustained formation experience. The app must not claim that Scripture commands a 77-day program, that a particular number guarantees transformation, or that every habit is established within this period.

The eleven themes provide an organized progression, not eleven ranks of Christianity. Someone reaching Week 11 has not been declared more holy than someone in Week 1.

## The first release and the longer-term vision

V1 is a complete personal experience: account access, setup, a current journey, all 77 days of reviewed formation content, the daily practices, private reflection, progress and history, optional reminders, account controls, and the reliable-use behavior described in this set.

Private communities are a committed future direction. Families, friends, Bible studies, small groups, and churches will eventually be able to participate together with encouragement and prayer. That future must protect personal writing and reject competition.

The app and all future features remain completely free. An optional way to support the app financially may be considered later, but no person, family, group, or church may be required to pay to participate or use a feature.

## How success should be understood

The product is useful when people understand what to do, can participate within their circumstances, can return without shame after an absence, and can trust the privacy and accuracy of their personal record.

Qualitative questions matter: Did the experience help the person engage Scripture? Did it support prayer and a concrete response in daily life? Did it encourage continuing beyond the program? Did the person understand that God's favor was not being earned through the app?

Participation counts can describe use. They cannot establish salvation, holiness, answered prayer, or the depth of a relationship with Jesus.

Numeric examples with a total of five assume two Chosen Practices. Actual daily totals reflect all assigned practices (5–7).
